# ClipHistory — product presentation script

Voice-over by a product manager, for Mac users who might download the app. About two minutes.
Every claim below matches the shipped app (v1.3 plus the fixes on `main`). There are no usage numbers or business
results in it, because we don't have any yet. Each sentence is one spoken line; the visuals in film.js are timed to
lines by their position in a section, so keep the line count per section when editing.

---

## 1. Opening
**On screen:** the ClipHistory icon, then the name.

Hi everyone.

Let me show you ClipHistory, a small Mac app for a problem we all live with.

## 2. The problem
**On screen:** one clipboard slot; each new copy pushes the last one out.

We copy things all day. A link. A line of code. A color, a screenshot.

But the Mac clipboard holds only one thing at a time. Copy something new… and the last thing is gone.

So we go back and search through tabs and chats to find it again. That's lost time, and lost focus.

## 3. The solution
**On screen:** the history list, "50", a pinned card moving to the top.

ClipHistory fixes that. It remembers what you copy, so nothing is lost.

Your last fifty items, newest first. And anything you pin stays as long as you want.

## 4. How it works
**On screen:** ⇧⌘V keys, the popup, the selection moving, Return.

It's simple. Press Shift, Command, V… and your history drops down from the top of the screen.

Use the arrow keys, press Return, and it's back on your clipboard, ready to paste. You never leave the app you're in.

## 5. Key features
**On screen:** search, category filters, pins, the desktop widget and the Services menu.

A few things make it fast.

Search. Just type, and the list narrows instantly.

Categories. Links, colors, images, code and text are sorted automatically.

Pins. What you paste every week stays at the top.

And it fits into macOS. A desktop widget shows your recent items, and you can paste from the right-click Services menu.

## 6. Privacy
**On screen:** a lime lock; secrets bounce off it; the retention picker.

Privacy is a core feature, not an extra. Your history is encrypted on disk, with the key in your Mac's keychain.

Password manager entries are skipped. API keys and card numbers are never saved.

You choose how long history is kept: a day, a week, a month, or forever. And there's no network code, so it all stays on your Mac.

## 7. Who it's for
**On screen:** writers and marketers, designers, developers.

Who is it for? Anyone who copies and pastes all day.

Writers and marketers, with their links and snippets. Designers, with colors and images. And developers, with the commands they reuse.

## 8. Value
**On screen:** three short lines.

The value is simple. Less searching, fewer interruptions, nothing lost.

## 9. Availability and close
**On screen:** icon, name, facts, "Download for Mac", the GitHub URL.

ClipHistory is free and open source, under the MIT license. It runs on macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

It's notarized by Apple, and uses Liquid Glass on macOS 26. Download it today from GitHub.

Give it a try. Thank you.
